How Bottle works

Distill the story beat

Bottle keeps the brief focused on the storytelling intent. Start with the subject and the emotion you need on screen — the layout guides you toward the details that steer diffusion systems best.

Dial the tone

Swap between cinematic, clean, atmospheric, playful, or experimental tones. Each preset nudges colour and lighting suggestions so your renders land closer to the art direction.

Shareable references

Export the generated copy blocks into your production notes, prompt history, or creative decks. Teams can remix bottles and rapidly explore alternate treatments.
