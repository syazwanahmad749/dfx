Storyboard pipeline

  1. Create a new Bottle for each key beat of the film.
  2. Export the generated copy into your story deck and attach frames rendered with your diffusion tool of choice.
  3. Share the Bottle set with editors to maintain continuity as they iterate on timing and pacing.

Look development

Use Bottle to capture lighting sweeps, lens treatments, and set dressing details. When you export the brief, the tone blocks become presets for your render queue, guaranteeing visual consistency across dozens of variations.
